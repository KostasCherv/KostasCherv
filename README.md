## 💫 About Me

I am a Senior Software Engineer based in Zurich, focused on building production ready backend systems, AI applications, data platforms, and Web3 infrastructure.

My work sits at the intersection of **AI**, **finance**, **blockchain**, and **backend engineering**. I enjoy turning complex technical problems into reliable systems that are practical, maintainable, and useful.

* 🤖 I design and build **agentic AI systems**, **RAG pipelines**, **LLM evaluation flows**, and **AI enabled backend tools**
* 🔭 I enjoy working on **developer friendly APIs**, **data heavy applications**, **automation systems**, and **financial analytics**
* 🧠 Deep interests: **LLMOps**, **agentic workflows**, **inference optimization**, **DeFi protocols**, **quantitative finance**, and **distributed system design**
* 🛠️ Main stack: **Python**, **TypeScript**, **Node.js**, **FastAPI**, **NestJS**, **PostgreSQL**, and **cloud infrastructure**
* 📊 I like working with **on chain data**, **financial data**, **dashboards**, and **decision support systems**
* 💬 Ask me about **AI backend architecture**, **RAG**, **agentic systems**, **Web3 infrastructure**, or **backend systems at scale**
* ⚡ Fun fact: I love turning messy on chain and financial data into something visual and actionable, preferably with a chart and a backtest
